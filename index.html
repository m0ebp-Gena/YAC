Introducing Yet Another Controller

Yet Another Controller (YAC) lets you operate a compatible amateur radio transceiver from your computer keyboard, with speech and braille feedback through your screen reader.

I'm M0EBP, and I continue to develop YAC with substantial help from AI.

What YAC can do

Check your signal strength, frequency and mode, or tune to another frequency. Depending on your radio, you can also adjust gain, squelch, power, filters, noise reduction, split, offsets and antenna selection.

YAC can fetch DX spots and tune to a selected station. Its decoder tools include CW, RTTY and FT8/FT4 reception on supported setups.
