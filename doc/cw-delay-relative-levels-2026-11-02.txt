CW Delay - relative Icom levels

Applied in D:\YAC on 2 November 2026 after explicit user approval.

Icom IC-705, IC-7300, IC-7300MK2, IC-7600 and IC-9700 now offer c d.
The command is CI-V 14 0F, with raw levels 0..255 encoded as packed BCD.
YAC announces Delay Level 1..16. Presets are:
1=0, 2=17, 3=34, 4=51, 5=68, 6=85, 7=102, 8=119,
9=136, 10=153, 11=170, 12=187, 13=204, 14=221, 15=238, 16=255.
These are relative adjustment levels, not milliseconds or dot lengths.
The speculative raw-to-dot and time conversions remain removed.

Opening reads the raw setting without writing or applying a default.
An intermediate raw value is announced as the nearest preset level.
Full stop selects the next strictly higher raw preset; comma the next
strictly lower. For example, raw 10 opens as Level 2, remains raw 10 on q,
and moves to raw 17 with full stop or raw 0 with comma.
The nearest announced level can therefore repeat after an intermediate
raw value moves to a preset. Both limits clamp without wraparound or writes.
Successful changes require positive acknowledgement and exact raw readback.
d reads again, h explains, q keeps the actual setting and returns to CW.
Keyer speed and Break-in mode are never changed.

Yaesu Delay remains in documented milliseconds with its existing step table.
Four-language labels, generic help and separate radio notes are aligned.
Physical-radio verification remains outstanding. No build or publication.

Verification: 24 focused CW, Delay and package-layout tests passed.
Checks include all 256 raw values, both adjustment directions, every preset,
both limits, all five Icom profiles and all four languages. Invalid BCD,
negative acknowledgements and mismatched readback cannot announce success.
Entry, repeat d, help and q preserve an intermediate raw value exactly.
