Radio Control Command List

Principles:
Messaging must be minimal as users using a braille display will have to see the full content of a line by scrolling the text. Those listening to a screen reader will be distracted from listening to the incoming signal. What is the point of an accessibility solution that degrades the activity it is attempting to support.

Thus distracting levels deep into layers should be avoided. The most used commands should be at the top level.

Logical single letters that are easily entered by a braille display using the American North computer code. This is a problem for translated languages. e.g. e for edit in English may not be a word that begins with e in German and  or Polish. Thus the logic is lost. Thus the action for yac is to comment upon this within the help documentation. In order to assist non English speakers of the reasoning for the key bindings.

Most commands use lowercase letters. Period and comma are also used as unshifted movement keys. Not needing to press Enter unless it is a level adjustment means that the bindings below are a starting point and may be changed during development and implementation.

(.) = Up; (,) = down. Outside a control, they adjust frequency by its configured step. Press q or another command key to finish a movement mode.
Where the scale is 255 as with AF / RF Gain 8/256 providing 32 increments. But clarify that before implementation.

Why should blind hams have and use a Windows PC? Mac and Linux are supported to, thus being coded in python at terminal level. While other controllers attempt to mimmic a screen reader by the use of modifier keys, this approach has not worked well for braille displays. More importantly, hams that have lost their sight later in life struggle to adapt from moving a mouse and clicking to holding several keys at once. Thus yac must maintain the simplicity of one key control.

This project aims to be published under the GNU Public License in the hope that others will take it forward. Especially in respect of non English language support. Furthermore, manufacturers may spair a little of their developer time to add their new radio releases to the code. So blind ham innovators can be confident that the first radios produced can be used by a blind ham.

Top Level Keys:

a AF Gain

b (Band up or down) using period & comma; period moves up, comma moves down. Press q or any other key to finish band changes. YAC reports the current band and frequency after each successful change.

e edit current frequency in MHz using a decimal point (for example 7.2)

o (Offset) cycles Receive, Transmit, Clear; period adds 10 Hz and comma subtracts 10 Hz for Receive or Transmit, either key clears in Clear mode. Press q or any other key to finish.


r RF Gain / Squelsh

t Tune the antenna. (Depending upon the tuner setting - internal / external tuner)

w Where Am I (Using Icom presentation, Signal, frequency and mode)

z (Zero round up or down the frequency to the nearest kilohertz of the currently used VFO.
