YAC 001 r7, 12 November 2026

FT8 and FT4 reception

Intel Mac and Linux x86-64 now have optional receive engines. In Settings,
press u to check for updates. YAC asks about its own update first and offers
the matching decoder when none is installed or configured. Press y to install
the decoder or n to skip. Apple Silicon and Linux ARM64 engines remain
available. Windows continues to use the bundled engine.

The Intel Mac engine was built and tested on macOS Big Sur 11.7.11. It uses
system libraries. The Linux x86-64 engine was built on Debian 13 and requires
glibc 2.39 or newer, plus the ALSA, udev, OpenSSL 3, GNU Fortran and
single-precision FFTW runtime libraries. The installer checks that the
engine can start. The Mac/Linux engine needs a playback device separate
from the radio input. Synchronize the computer clock before decoding.

Save the radio input with d, a. Use full stop or comma to browse and
Enter to save. A callsign and Maidenhead locator are needed.

From Top Level, press d, f to open the decoder. With a connected radio,
YAC tunes to the selected digital mode's preset on the current band and
starts receiving. In Demo Mode, tune the physical radio manually.

Inside the decoder, b opens the band presets and e enters a frequency
in MHz. m opens Radio Mode, and w reads signal, frequency and radio mode.
d opens Digital Mode. Use full stop or comma to choose FT8 or FT4.
The radio follows the new mode's preset for the current band. If there
is no preset, enter a frequency with e and start with s.

Press v to read the latest decode. Comma reads the previous message
and full stop reads the next. A usable locator gives an estimated
distance. s stops or starts decoding, and q leaves the decoder.

The CW menu also offers Radio Mode with m. b remains Break-in.

Verification

On Debian 13 x86-64, the FTX-1 tuned to the 40 m FT8 preset on entry,
then to the 40 m FT4 preset when the digital mode changed. Both modes
decoded live signals and displayed distances. The Intel Mac engine
decoded live FT8 in Demo Mode. Live tuning on other bands was not tested.
