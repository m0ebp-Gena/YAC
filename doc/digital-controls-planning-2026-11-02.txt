Digital controls planning - 2 November 2026

User suggestion
The existing Decode submenu may be able to accommodate the additional
D-Star and C4FM controls. Consider this location when designing digital
operation and repeater programming. This is a possible location, not an
agreed menu layout or authorization to implement controls.

Current Decode submenu letters
a Audio setup
c CW decoding
h Help
q Return to top level
r RTTY decoding

Inside decoder views, s, v, n and z also have functions. Preserve these
existing controls when considering additions.

Digital scope to investigate
IC-705: DV mode, separate DR function, station and routing callsigns,
digital squelch, and repeater frequency/duplex/offset settings.
FTX-1: C4FM-DN/VW, AMS, transmit/receive DG-ID, station identity,
repeater settings and optional WIRES-X operation.
Confirm CAT/CI-V read and write support before proposing controls.
