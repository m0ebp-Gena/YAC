CW Delay correction - milliseconds only

Applied in D:\YAC on 2 November 2026 with user approval.
This supersedes the earlier approximate Icom implementation.

The speculative Icom raw-code-to-dot tables and the approximation
announcement have been removed, including their unused language labels.
Delay is exposed only on models with a documented milliseconds mapping:
FT-DX10, FT-710 and FTX-1. Their existing controls keep the same sequence:
c d reads current milliseconds; full stop/comma adjust; d rereads;
h explains; q keeps the value and returns to CW. No wrapping, defaults,
keyer-speed changes or Break-in mode changes.

IC-705, IC-7300, IC-7300MK2, IC-7600 and IC-9700 Delay now report
Not Supported without sending CAT/CI-V commands. Their existing Break-in
support is unchanged. A future implementation requires a reliable model
raw-code-to-dot mapping, a live keyer-speed reading, conversion to integer
milliseconds and verified native readback. If speed changes, the equivalent
milliseconds for a dot-based delay may change.

Hamlib defines BKINDL in tenths of dots and BKIN_DLYMS in integer
milliseconds. Its PARIS timing conversion uses 1200/WPM milliseconds per
dot. This time conversion does not establish an Icom raw-code mapping.
