QMX identification and signal readback

Implemented 4 November 2026.

CAT ID020 alone does not identify a QMX or a TS-480. After discovery,
including saved-port discovery, YAC queries VN; without changing settings.
A valid firmware reply containing QMX confirms QRP Labs QMX; QMX+ confirms
QRP Labs QMX Plus. The identity is retained for that connection and displayed
as the connected radio. Rejected or unrecognised replies retain the existing
connection description and meter handling.

For a confirmed QMX, the normal w report queries SM; rather than SM0;.
It displays the returned dB value without an unverified conversion to S units.
Signed values use the selected language's Plus or Minus label.
An unreadable meter still reports S ?.

Evidence: the user's log in Latest/yac-win-test-2026-11-03-t1/logs/
yac-debug.log repeatedly shows SM0; rejected with ?; on COM6 on 4 November.
Manufacturer command reference:
https://www.qrp-labs.com/images/qmx/manuals/cat_1_04_004.pdf

Verification: mocked positive QMX/QMX+ identification, rejected and malformed
identification replies, QMX signal parsing and failed reads, and unchanged
TS-480 signal query. Physical QMX SM; readback remains to be tested.
