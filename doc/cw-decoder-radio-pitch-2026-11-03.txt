CW decoder controls and radio pitch - 3 November 2026

Audio decoding reads the current radio CW pitch before opening capture and
uses that value instead of a fixed or saved decoder pitch. During reception,
it checks the pitch with the existing receive-state polling and follows
changes silently. No pitch-setting commands are sent to the radio. There is
no decoder p control. WPM remains adjustable silently using comma and period.

An unreadable pitch prevents startup. If pitch readback fails during capture,
decoding pauses and retries on the next receive-state check. It resumes after
valid readback. Native radio CW decoding is unchanged.

s starts or stops decoding. v announces signal, frequency and selected WPM.
r opens Review; h displays the same CW-only help in CW and Review. q in
Review returns to CW; q in CW stops capture and leaves. Text is retained
until cleared or YAC closes, up to 10,000 characters. c clears text in Review.
