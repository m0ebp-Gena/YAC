Microphone gain - current implementation, 2 November 2026

s m opens directly, reading mode and current gain without a setting write.
Full stop/comma adjust in receive, v reads, h gives mode-dependent help,
q finishes with Done and keeps the value. A mode change or unreadable mode
ends the control before further adjustments. YAC never keys transmission.

SSB offers optional ALC preparation on Space, with y to start or n/q to
cancel preparation and retain direct adjustment. Space resets the peak once
monitoring is active. Yaesu AM also offers optional ALC. Other AM, FM and
DV use speech-quality guidance and do not sample ALC. Space repeats help.
ALC is sampled, raw and uncalibrated; it cannot judge modulation quality.
Kenwood meter selection is prepared only on explicit ALC monitoring start
and retains existing checked restoration and pending-restoration handling.

Model/mode command paths are in each model's mic-gain.json; language
labels and h008/h049/h050/h051 supply mode-specific presentation. See
English/mic-gain-radio-notes.txt and its German, Spanish and Polish peers
for model coverage, including Kenwood's separate FM three-level menus.
These files are included in the package document mapping.
