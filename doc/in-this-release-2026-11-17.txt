YAC 001 r4, 17 November 2026

DX spots

From Top Level, press j for recent DX spots. YAC sends your saved
callsign to the DX cluster and lists up to ten spots, newest first.
Use full stop and comma to browse. Press Enter to tune to the spot
and apply its reported or likely mode if the radio supports it.
Press r to refresh, m to select the radio mode manually, h for help,
or q to return to Top Level.

Automatic radio power

From Top Level, press s, o. The control announces the saved setting.
Press o or full stop to advance, or comma to go back. The choices
are Off, Start only, Stop only, and Start and stop. Press q to keep
the choice and return to Settings. The default is Off.

Start wakes the selected saved radio at launch if the radio supports
wake-up and its control port is visible. Stop sends the selected
radio a power-off command after you confirm quitting YAC, where
the radio supports that command.

Windows radio startup check

In the Windows r3 hardware test, YAC detected the FTX-1 on COM22 on
two launches. On two other
launches it woke the FTDX10 on COM9 and connected. Both radios
returned frequency, mode and signal readings. The log also records
power-off requests for both radios.
