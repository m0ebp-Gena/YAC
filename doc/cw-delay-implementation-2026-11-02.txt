Agreed operation:
c d reads and announces the current delay without changing it.
Full stop increases to the next value; comma decreases to the previous.
Changes apply immediately and require matching native readback.
At minimum and maximum, further presses repeat that value without a write.
There is no wraparound. d reads again without changing the value.
h gives generic help. q keeps the choice, says Done and returns to CW.
Another q announces Top Level. No defaults or Break-in mode changes.
