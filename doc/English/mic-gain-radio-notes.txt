Microphone gain - radio notes

s m reads the current mode and gain. Full stop/comma adjust immediately
while receiving; v reads, h gives help for that mode, q keeps the setting.
No Space is required for gain adjustment on any supported radio.

Yaesu FT-DX10, FT-710 and FTX-1 use MG, 0-100, in supported SSB, AM and
FM modes (including narrow AM/FM). FTX-1 also offers the same command path
in C4FM-DN/VW. Confirm audible effect and any shared settings on hardware.
SSB and AM offer optional sampled ALC feedback using Space, preparation,
and y. The FTX-1 transmit monitor does not operate in FM/FM-N or AM/AM-N;
use another station or suitable external monitoring for those modes.

Icom IC-705, IC-7300, IC-7300MK2, IC-7600 and IC-9700 use CI-V 14 0B,
raw 0-255, shown as rounded percentages. Steps are 3 raw units. SSB, AM
and FM are supported; IC-705 and IC-9700 also support DV. SSB offers
optional ALC feedback. For AM, FM and DV, check speech clarity using a
supported monitor or another station; no ALC judgement is made.

Kenwood TS-590SG and positively identified TS-480 use MG 0-100 for SSB
and AM. FM instead uses a separate three-level setting: Normal, Medium,
High. TS-590SG menu 53 uses EX0530000; TS-480 menu 44 uses EX0440000.
CAT values 0, 1, 2 correspond to those three displayed choices. Full stop
and comma select adjacent levels without wrapping. SSB offers optional
ALC feedback; AM/FM use speech-quality checks. Meter selection is only
prepared after explicitly starting ALC feedback. QMX is not enabled by
its shared ID020 identification.

These are command-path and automated checks, not physical validation of
modulation quality, deviation, shared gain storage or digital speech.
