Filter navigation review - 4 November 2026

Required sequence
Press f, then the filter key. Entry reads the current setting without
changing it. Toggle or adjust as supported. Press q to keep the setting
and finish the adjustment with Done, remaining in Filters. Another filter
can then be opened directly. Press q from Filters to return to Top Level.

Reviewed controls
a Auto Notch, b Noise Blanker, c Contour, l Low Cut, n Manual Notch,
p Bandwidth, r Noise Reduction, and u Upper Cut. Included Contour frequency,
level and width, Manual Notch width, and separate bandwidth edges.

Finding and correction
Noise Reduction already followed the required q sequence. Bandwidth cleared
its active adjustment state when its profile was unsupported or unreadable.
The next q left Filters, and adjustment keys could be forwarded to Top Level.
Bandwidth now retains its active control until q, consistently with the other
filters. Bandwidth help is updated in all four languages.

Verification
68 related automated tests passed. Navigation checks cover all eight filter
keys in English, German, Spanish and Polish, entry without writes, toggles,
adjustments, active filters, help, verification, unsupported and unreadable
responses, and opening another filter after q. q preserves settings.
The new regression checks reproduced the Bandwidth failure before the fix.
Testing used the dummy radio and simulated native responses; physical radio
verification remains pending. No new user package was built for this change.
