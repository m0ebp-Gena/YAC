AF and RF gain have separate adjustable steps. Press s inside either
control to enter a step in percentage points. The step saves on Enter.

Top Level, CW and Decoders help give short overviews. Individual
controls have shorter help. Settings, i reads radio information and
returns to Settings. SSB microphone gain help gives seven steps for
checking ALC and adjusting gain.

Initial Elecraft KX2 support includes CAT identification, tuning,
band and mode selection, receive gain, preamplifier, attenuator,
dial lock, keyer speed and noise reduction on/off. KX2 checks used
simulated replies. Physical-radio verification is pending.
