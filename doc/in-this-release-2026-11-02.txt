CONTROL ENTRY AND VERIFICATION
Selectors and filters read their current setting on entry without changing
it. Off functions report Off without an inactive adjustment value. Changed
values are applied by explicit subsequent keys. Verify reports active locks;
Lock Off is omitted, and an unreadable supported lock reports Unknown.
Offset separates Receive/Transmit selection, explicit Clear and Verify.

CW
c opens CW: b Break-in; d Delay; i Internal Keyer; k Speed; p Pitch;
v Sidetone Volume. h gives contextual help; q keeps applied settings.
Break-in combines Off, Semi and Full where supported. Internal Keyer entry
reads state; another i toggles it. Delay uses milliseconds for supported
Yaesu radios and relative levels 1-16 for supported Icom radios, with no
unverified conversion to time. Speed accepts at most two digits; h and q
work immediately without Enter. Pitch and volume use full stop/comma.
FTX-1/FT-DX10/FT-710 sidetone volume selects CW-U only when needed and
keeps CW on exit; an existing CW-U/CW-L selection is preserved.
Separate language-specific radio notes explain model coverage and limits.

MICROPHONE GAIN
s m selects the control and help for the current voice mode. Full stop and
comma adjust while receiving; v reads; h explains; q keeps the gain.
Opening never changes mode or gain. Space is not needed for adjustment.
Space offers optional ALC preparation in SSB and supported Yaesu AM.
FM and digital voice use speech-quality guidance without ALC assessment.
Kenwood FM uses its separate Normal/Medium/High menu, not the SSB gain.
Mode changes end adjustment. YAC never keys transmission for this control.
English, German, Spanish and Polish help and notes are included.

DECODER AND PROCESSOR
Includes current decoder/audio-setup, filter, speech-processor and control
entry corrections. Optional audio CW decoding remains experimental.

WINDOWS UPDATES
Retains same-volume staging/rollback so an installation on D: can update
when Windows Temp is on C:. Old updaters may require a fresh full package
or the documented one-time same-drive Temp workaround to receive that fix.
Both current and legacy-compatible download assets are supplied.
