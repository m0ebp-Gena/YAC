GET SWR
=======

Press g at the top level. Connect an antenna suitable for the current frequency
and check the frequency is clear. Press g again to measure, h for help or q to
return. Entering the control does not transmit. Each measurement needs another
g; there is no automatic repetition.

YAC temporarily selects the model's carrier mode and measurement power. It
allows a short settling interval and collects up to three readings within a
nominal one-second transmit window,
requests receive, verifies receive, restores the saved settings and then
announces the result. A slow or disconnected serial link can delay the receive
request; the one-second window is not a hardware transmitter watchdog.

The response is SWR approx followed by the ratio, or SWR at least followed by
the top of the calibrated range. These are meter estimates, not an assessment
of the antenna or a prediction of whether a tuner will match it. No antenna
tuner cycle is requested, and tuner selection is preserved.

No valid RF, absent/unstable meter readings or insufficient measurement power
produce SWR unavailable. Invalid means the frequency or context cannot be used.
Memory, split, repeater shift, active offsets, scanning and unreadable context
are refused. Use a normal simplex VFO. YAC does not change region or broaden
the radio's transmit capabilities.

Settings restoration
--------------------
YAC saves the native power setting, current frequency and mode. Frequency
identifies the band; no band-change command or VFO swap is sent. Icom mode,
data-mode state and filter selection are saved together. Carrier-mode power
and mic-gain slots, VOX and any changed modulation-source settings are restored
as well. The other VFO is not written.

Receive is requested before restoring a user's higher configured power.
Ctrl+C and measurement errors also run this restoration path. If receive cannot
be confirmed, YAC says RX unconfirmed and retains the measurement settings.
If a setting cannot be restored/read back, YAC says Restore failed. The saved
transaction remains in memory. Use g again to retry restoration; other changing
commands are blocked until it succeeds. YAC also retries before closing the
radio on exit. Physical disconnection can prevent restoration; a successful
result is never announced in its place.

Model profiles
--------------
FT-DX10 and FT-710: unmodulated AM carrier, 5 W. AM source is temporarily MIC,
with microphone gain zero; the DX10's AM-specific gain is also saved/muted.
The model manuals do not prescribe a higher SWR-specific measurement power.
SWR conversion uses the approximate Hamlib model tables. Those tables were
derived from related-radio measurements and are not a physical YAC calibration.

FTX-1: unmodulated AM; 1 W on the readable Field-head power selector and 5 W
with SPA-1 selected. The original selector is preserved. Hamlib's FTX-1 SWR
table explicitly remains provisional; do not describe it as hardware-verified.

IC-7300, IC-7300MK2 and IC-7600: RTTY, requesting 35 W, with an approximate RF
meter check above 30 W. This follows the manufacturer's RTTY/30-W measurement
guidance rather than the earlier proposed universal 5-W setting.

IC-705: RTTY, requesting 6 W, with an RF meter check above 5 W. The manual
requires more than 5 W, so battery-only operation cannot run this measurement.

IC-9700: RTTY; 30 W on 144/430 MHz and 3 W on 1200 MHz, checked against the
corresponding approximate RF meter reading. The original native power value
is restored, not a rounded conversion back from watts.

TS-590SG: unmodulated AM, 5 W. Manufacturer VOICE3 SWR-dot announcement bins
are used instead of Hamlib's differing interpolation. A zero/no-numeric bin
is unavailable; the manufacturer's OVER bin is reported as at least 5.

TS-480HX/SAT: numerical measurement is currently disabled. The manufacturer
CAT reference describes 0-10 meter dots, whereas Hamlib's SWR calibration uses
0-20. This disagreement must be resolved before reporting a numerical ratio.

QMX/QMX Plus: not enabled. SW reports SWR directly, but PC is measured power,
not a watt-setting command. A verified reversible carrier/power sequence has
not been established. YAC must not send TS-480 commands merely because these
radios share ID020.

Region and resources
--------------------
Use s r to choose the region for the radio's location; Region 1 is the default.
The regional gate excludes air/marine frequencies and unsupported regional
segments. Current regional foundations do not enable automatic RF on the
country-dependent 60-m and 4-m allocations. National rules still apply.

Model procedures, native commands, calibration data and reference URLs are in
each model's swr.json under rad_cmds. The measurement module, native transport,
regional tables and all four language resources are separate. Sources include:

Validation status
-----------------
Native CAT/CI-V emulator tests cover preparation, confirmed RF, conversion,
USB/LSB and mode-slot restoration, cancellation, failed preparation, lost
readback, uncertain RX, restoration retry and regional rejection. No physical
radio was keyed during development. No visual meter comparison is required
from a blind operator; remaining calibration limitations are recorded above.
