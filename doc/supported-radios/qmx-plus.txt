QRP Labs QMX Plus
=================

PURPOSE AND MANUFACTURER GUIDANCE
This file supplements the radio manufacturer's user guide; it does not replace
it. Consult that guide for the complete procedure, precautions and details for
your model, firmware, microphone and operating mode. Manufacturer references
are listed below.

YAC exists to remove visual barriers. For microphone adjustment, its purpose
is to make the gain setting and transmitter meter feedback available through
speech and braille, so the user can follow the manufacturer's procedure and
make their own adjustments without having to inspect the radio display.
Accessible guidance should explain a reading where the model-specific mapping
has been verified; unverified estimates must remain clearly identified.

BROAD GUIDANCE - USER CHOOSES WHETHER TO TRY IT
Prepared 14 October 2026 for YAC beta testing.

These notes are a starting point, not a calibrated pass/fail test. Read the
manufacturer manual for your exact model, firmware, microphone and mode.
YAC support does not mean every feature has been tested on physical hardware.

Before any transmitting test, connect an antenna correctly matched for the
test frequency or a suitable dummy load. An unsuitable load can damage the
transmitter. YAC cannot verify the load. Lower power does not make it safe.
Set an appropriate RF power yourself and speak normally. Shouting is not
necessary and can overdrive the audio. Keep PTT under your control.

ALC is transmitter Automatic Level Control. AGC normally concerns receiver
Automatic Gain Control. Mic gain and speech processing are separate controls.
A raw ALC number is neither watts nor a microphone-gain percentage.

YAC s m changes only mic gain when you press full stop or comma. It does not
change RF power, frequency, mode or speech processing and never keys TX.
For basic adjustment, turn the speech processor/compressor off yourself.
Hold PTT and speak normally, then release it to hear the sampled peak ALC.
Brief peaks may fall between samples. No Good/Low/High judgement is currently
made. An unavailable reading is unknown, not zero. Restore any preparation
settings you changed yourself when finished.

MICROPHONE GUIDANCE AND YAC LIMITATION
YAC has partial QMX-family support, including existing filter handling.
The new s m module is not enabled for QMX or QMX Plus. Sharing CAT ID020
with the TS-480 does not establish compatible mic-gain or ALC commands.

The QRP Labs operating manual for firmware 1_03_002 describes a microphone
test tool displaying average and peak audio amplitude. It adjusts microphone
gain in dB and suggests normal speech peaks reaching the tool's red region.
That is microphone-audio guidance, not a Yaesu/Icom ALC target. Use the manual
for your installed firmware; do not apply the 36-60 or 65-70 numbers here.

YAC microphone control, meter interpretation and physical validation for these
radios require separate work. Do not try to force a TS-480 identification.

AGC - RECEIVER GAIN RECOVERY
YAC s a AGC control is not implemented for this profile. Do not use TS-480 commands solely because CAT ID020 is shared.
Fast recovers sooner after a strong signal; Slow holds gain reduction longer.
Choose by listening and consult the manufacturer user guide. Off disables
automatic gain regulation, so received audio may vary more widely. No
transmission is required. Physical-radio validation remains pending.

SPEECH PROCESSOR - DEVELOPMENT BETA
Speech Processor s e remains unavailable for this profile; sharing TS-480 CAT ID020 is not sufficient.

SETTINGS HELP - MODEL DETAILS
AGC adjustment is not implemented for this model. Unknown readings prevent AGC changes; rejected or unconfirmed changes report failure. See in-command h for the operating procedure and feedback.
