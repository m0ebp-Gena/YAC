Elecraft KX2

Connect the radio's ACC port to the computer using the Elecraft USB serial
interface cable, then turn on the radio and start YAC. YAC probes the serial
connection and confirms the KX2 using its model-specific CAT option reply.

YAC controls VFO A frequency, tuning steps, band and mode selection, AF and
RF gain, FM squelch, preamplifier, attenuator, VFO A lock and CW keyer speed.
Use Settings, then i, for radio information and MCU firmware. Verify status
reports the configured power setting in watts. The signal readout reports
the raw CAT meter value.

In Filters, r reads the noise reduction state. Press r again to switch it
on or off, and q to return to Filters. YAC verifies the state using the
radio display response. Level adjustment still requires the radio control.
Noise reduction requires automatic gain control and is unavailable in DATA.

Frequency entry covers 0.5 to 32 MHz. Band Select covers the 80 to 10 metre
amateur bands. Keyer speed uses 8 to 50 WPM. FM selection requires MCU 2.89 or
later and FM MODE enabled in the radio menu. AM selection depends on the
radio's mode configuration.

Power editing, split, offsets, other filters, native decoding, tuner operation and
SWR measurement require further integration. Initial verification uses
simulated CAT replies. Physical-radio verification is pending.
